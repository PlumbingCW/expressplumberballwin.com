A failed tank: stop the water, then call

When a tank fails, the problem is rarely the heater alone. A 40 or 50 gallon tank can release its full volume plus the line pressure behind it, and the water will keep coming until the supply is closed. Close the cold valve above the heater first. If the valve will not turn, close the main shutoff where the water line enters the house.

Then cut power at the breaker for an electric unit or turn the gas valve to off for a gas unit. Soaked insulation and drywall dry faster when you start early, so towels and a wet vacuum help while you wait. A repair may still be possible, and the plumber confirms that on site, which is covered on the water heater repair page.

Many homeowners choose a like-for-like swap in an emergency because it is the fastest path back to hot water. Changing fuel type or moving to tankless is a bigger job. It can be scheduled after the emergency is over, and tankless water heater service explains what that involves.

What the swap involves, step by step

  1. 01

    Shut off and drain

    Water, power or gas are isolated and the tank is drained to a floor drain or outdoors.

  2. 02

    Remove the old unit

    Lines, flue and relief piping are disconnected and the tank is taken away.

  3. 03

    Set and connect

    The new tank is leveled, lines are connected with new flex connectors and a relief valve line is run.

  4. 04

    Test and explain

    The plumber checks for leaks, vent draft or electrical load, and sets a safe temperature.

Sizing and the permit question

Tank size is set by how many people shower in the morning, not by the square footage of the house. A two-person household often does fine with 40 gallons, while a family of five usually wants 50 or more. A plumber can read the label on the old tank and match or adjust it.

Permit rules differ from one St. Louis County city to the next, and we do not guess at them. Ballwin lists a Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129, and the city says occupied buildings must meet its housing code. Ask them before the work if you are selling the house.

Hard water and the life of your next heater

Missouri American Water reports total hardness of 66 to 226 ppm as calcium carbonate in its 2023 St. Louis County report, which is moderately hard to hard water. Scale shortens the life of any heater. An anode rod check every few years and a yearly flush are the simplest ways to stretch the next one, and a water softener reduces the load.

Standards note: The IPC also sets rules for relief valve discharge and, where required, drain pans, and we install to those rules. The City of Ballwin lists the International Plumbing Code among its adopted codes, and the Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 can confirm which edition applies.
