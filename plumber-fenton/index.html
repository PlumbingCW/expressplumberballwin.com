Fenton sits on the west bank of the Meramec

Fenton lies along the west bank of the Meramec River, about two miles south of Interstate 44. The city was founded in 1818, was incorporated in 1948 and counted 3,989 residents in 2020. Its parks include Fenton City Park and the Fenton Meramec Greenway, and Old Towne Fenton is the original eight-square-block core.

River towns have a particular plumbing story. Groundwater rises after heavy rain, sump pits work harder, and floodwater can overwhelm a sewer line. The U.S. Geological Survey has published flood inundation maps for the Meramec River at Valley Park and Fenton, which is a useful reference if you are not sure how your lot sits.
