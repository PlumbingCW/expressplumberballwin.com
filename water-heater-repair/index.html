No hot water or a leak: what to do in the first ten minutes

A water heater that has stopped heating is an inconvenience. One that is leaking is a flood waiting to happen, and a leak from the tank body itself usually means the unit is at the end of its life. Look at where the water is coming from before you decide which kind of call this is.

While you wait for the plumber

  • ✓Close the cold water supply valve at the top of the tank to stop the refill
  • ✓On an electric unit, switch off the breaker so the elements do not run dry
  • ✓On a gas unit, turn the gas control to pilot or off, unless you smell gas, in which case leave and call 911
  • ✓Move boxes and stored items away from the base of the heater
  • ✓Take a photo of any visible damage in case you file an insurance claim

A hot water heater thermostat or heating element is a modest repair when the tank is sound. Both are common parts that a plumber carries on the truck, which is why many electric units are back in service the same visit.

Why Ballwin water leaves sediment in tanks

Missouri American Water's 2023 water quality report for the St. Louis County system lists total hardness between 66 and 226 ppm as calcium carbonate, depending on whether the water came from a Meramec River plant or a Missouri River plant. By simple conversion that is roughly 4 to 13 grains per gallon. Hard water leaves mineral scale on heating elements and a layer of sediment at the bottom of a tank.

That scale is why elements burn out and why older tanks rumble or pop when they heat. A tank flush and a new element often restore a unit that is under ten years old. For a heater past that age, we tell you plainly when water heater replacement makes more sense than a third repair.

When repair stops making sense

A leak from a fitting or the relief valve is repairable. A leak from the tank shell is not, because the steel has corroded through. Age, rust-colored water and repeated element failures all point toward replacement.

If the unit is a tankless model, scale is the usual cause of error codes, and a descaling flush is part of tankless water heater service. We give you the repair cost and the replacement cost side by side, and the decision is yours.
