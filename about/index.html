About Ballwin Express Plumbing

Ballwin Express Plumbing is a local emergency plumbing dispatch service based at 14346 Manchester Road in Ballwin, MO. We answer the phone around the clock and send licensed plumbers to homes and businesses in Ballwin and nearby towns.

This page explains what we do, how a call works and how to check any contractor you hire.

Call (314) 461-6239

What we do

We take calls for burst pipes, sewer backups, water heater failures, clogged drains, leaks and planned plumbing work. The dispatcher listens, asks a few questions and sends a plumber with the right equipment. The plumber explains the repair and the price before work starts.

We keep the model simple on purpose. There are no web forms, no hidden fees and no pressure. You call (314) 461-6239, you talk to a person and you decide whether to go ahead.

How a call works

  1. 01

    You call

    A dispatcher answers, day or night, and asks where the problem is and what you have already done.

  2. 02

    A plumber is assigned

    Same-day visits are offered when a crew is free.

  3. 03

    You approve the price

    The plumber explains the work and the cost first.

  4. 04

    The repair is done

    You receive advice on prevention and, for water damage, on documentation for insurance.

Where we work

Our office is on Manchester Road, the main commercial street through Ballwin. From there our crews reach Ballwin, Ellisville, Manchester, Town and Country and Fenton. See the service areas page for ZIP codes and local notes.

Ballwin incorporated in 1950 and grew quickly through the 1960s and 1980s, according to census counts. Houses from those decades are common here, and so are the pipes, water heaters and sewer laterals that came with them. We plan each visit around the age of the home.

How to check any plumber you hire

Questions worth asking every contractor

  • ✓Do you hold a City of Ballwin contractor license and an active St. Louis County license in your trade?
  • ✓Can you show proof of workers' compensation insurance or an exemption affidavit?
  • ✓Will you state the price before starting work?
  • ✓Who pulls the permit if one is needed?
  • ✓Can I see the camera footage or photos of the problem?

The City of Ballwin lists these licensing and insurance requirements on its contractor license page, and the Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 can answer questions about permits. We encourage you to ask us the same questions.

Why a dispatch line works for plumbing emergencies

Most plumbing emergencies come down to a few questions. Is water still flowing, is sewage involved, and is anyone at risk? A trained dispatcher who answers those questions in the first minute can send the right crew, with the right equipment, and can coach you through shutting off the water while the plumber drives over.

That is the whole idea behind the service. You get a person who listens, a plumber who explains the work and a price you approve first. Our burst pipe, sewer line and water heater pages show what that looks like for the three calls we hear most.

The neighbors we serve

Ballwin shares borders and roads with Ellisville and Manchester, so many customers live a few minutes from our office. Town and Country and Fenton are farther out, and the dispatcher gives you an honest arrival estimate rather than a promise we cannot keep.

Response time depends on the time of day, the weather and where the crew is. During a hard freeze or a heavy storm, many homes need help at once, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly if the wait is longer than usual. In that case the first-ten-minutes steps on each emergency page matter even more, because stopping the water yourself limits the damage until the plumber arrives.

What we will not claim

We do not publish review counts, star ratings or awards on this site. We do not list license numbers we cannot show you in person, and we do not guess at prices without seeing the problem. If we do not know something, we say so, and we send you to the utility or the city that does.

What to do in an emergency, and where our facts come from

Our guides and service pages cite local sources, including Missouri American Water for water quality and cold-weather advice, the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District for sewer ownership, Spire for gas emergencies and the City of Ballwin for permits and its Sewer Lateral Repair Program. Where a fact could change, we point you to the source.
