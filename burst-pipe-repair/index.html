Pipe burst: stop the water before anything else

A burst supply pipe can release gallons a minute. Every minute counts, so find the main shutoff first. It is usually in the basement near the front wall, near the water heater, or at the meter, and it is either a lever or a round wheel.

In the first ten minutes

  • ✓Close the main water valve, turning a wheel clockwise or a lever a quarter turn
  • ✓Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the pressure
  • ✓Switch off the breaker for any room where water is near outlets or the ceiling is sagging
  • ✓Move furniture and valuables out of the wet area
  • ✓Photograph the damage before you clean up
  • ✓Call (314) 461-6239 and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from

If you cannot find or turn the main valve, call us anyway. The dispatcher can describe the likely spots, and a plumber can shut off at the meter if the valve has seized. Missouri American Water runs a 24/7 line at 1 (866) 269-2837 for water main and meter questions.

Why pipes burst in Ballwin homes

Common causes of a burst pipe
CauseWhere it happensTypical repair
FreezingExterior walls, crawlspaces, garages, unheated roomsReplace the split section and insulate
CorrosionOlder galvanized or aged copper linesReplace the section or repipe
High pressureHomes with no pressure regulatorRepair and fit a regulator
Joint failureFittings behind walls or ceilingsRe-make the joint
Physical damageAfter remodeling or drillingReplace the damaged piece

Pipes in outside walls and unheated garages freeze first during a cold snap. Ice blocks the line, and the burst usually shows after it thaws. For repeated problems, repiping a worn section can be cheaper than repeated repairs.

What the repair involves

  1. 01

    Isolate

    The plumber confirms the water is off and closes zone valves to keep the rest of the house in service.

  2. 02

    Open and locate

    A small opening is cut at the leak. Moisture readings show the extent of the wet area.

  3. 03

    Repair

    The damaged section is cut out and new pipe is installed with proper fittings.

  4. 04

    Test and advise

    The line is pressurized and checked, and you get advice on drying and insurance paperwork.

If the pipe is hidden and you do not know where the water is, leak detection can pinpoint the source. Drywall and insulation that stay wet for more than a day or two can grow mold, so drying matters as much as the pipe.

Insurance and permits

A sudden burst pipe is commonly covered by homeowner insurance for the resulting damage, but a slow leak often is not. Your adjuster will ask for photos, the plumber's invoice and the date. Call your insurer promptly and keep damaged materials until they have looked at them.

Ask the Ballwin Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 whether the repair needs a permit. Simple section replacements often do not, and larger repiping work may.
