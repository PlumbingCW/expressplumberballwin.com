A subdivision built in sections

Listings for homes here carry legal descriptions such as Rolling Hills Country Club Park Sec 4, which shows the subdivision was platted in numbered sections. A sampled Sec 4 listing on Far Hill Drive shows a 1985 build year and a contemporary style on a small lot of under a tenth of an acre. Other listings place homes on Rustic Valley Drive, Down Hill Drive and Rolling Glen Lane, all in ZIP code 63021.

Listings describe a community pool and note the area is close to Castlewood State Park and in the Parkway School District. Because sections were platted at different times, we do not give one age for the whole subdivision. Your parcel record shows the year your house was built.

Why section age matters for a plumber

Homes from different sections can have different fixtures, heater types and drain layouts. When a drain slows, the first question is whether it is one fixture or the whole house. When water appears, the second question is where the pipes run. Smaller lots and contemporary layouts can put the water heater in a closet or utility room instead of a basement, which changes how a leak spreads.

We do not assume a plumbing problem from the neighborhood name. We look at the house. For a hidden leak, see leak detection. For slow or gurgling drains, see drain cleaning.

  1. 01

    Locate the shutoff

    Find your main water valve, and any shutoff at the water heater, before an emergency.

  2. 02

    Check the label

    Look at the water heater's date label and at its base for moisture or rust.

  3. 03

    Watch the meter

    If every tap is off and the meter still moves, you may have a leak between the meter and the house.

Who owns what

MSD splits the responsibility this way: the public main is MSD's, and the private building sewer from your house to that main is the owner's. If neighbors are affected, MSD's number is 1 (866) 281-5737.

The City of Ballwin's Sewer Lateral Repair Program applies city-wide rules, not neighborhood ones: a $28 annual fee on tax bills pays for it, applicants pay $150, and each job is capped at $4,500. Call Inspections at (636) 227-2129 to check whether your address and problem qualify.

Hard water and fixtures

The 2023 Missouri American Water report for St. Louis County lists total hardness from 66 to 226 ppm as calcium carbonate. If you are a customer, scale is one reason a showerhead or aerator loses flow. Scale can slow a showerhead long before it damages a pipe.
