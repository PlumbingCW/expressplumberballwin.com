What a planned installation includes

A clean installation is mostly the work around the heater. The tank sits on a level base or inside a drain pan, the cold and hot lines connect through new flexible connectors, and a temperature and pressure relief valve discharges through a pipe to a safe spot. On gas units the flue connects with the right slope and clearance, and the gas line gets a sediment trap and a shutoff.

An expansion tank matters in homes where a pressure-reducing valve or backflow device closes the system. Heated water expands, and with nowhere to go the pressure climbs and shortens the life of the heater and fixtures. The plumber checks static pressure and tells you whether a tank is advised.

Permits and inspections in Ballwin

The City of Ballwin states that building permits are required for all kinds of construction and that plumbing permits are typically needed with basement finishing. It does not list water heaters on that page, so we do not assume either way. Call the Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 and ask whether your install needs one.

Contractors working in the city must hold a Ballwin contractor license plus an active St. Louis County license in their trade, according to the city's contractor license page. The city also notes that it has inspection and certification requirements for occupied buildings. If you are selling, get the installer's paperwork in your file.

Sizing and efficiency choices

Pick the size by the busiest hour of your day. Two showers plus a dishwasher at the same time is the real test, not the tank's advertised capacity. First-hour rating on the yellow label tells you how much hot water the unit delivers in that hour.

Heat pump water heaters pull heat from the surrounding air, so they need open space and a spot with a drain nearby. They suit a large unfinished basement. They are a poor fit for a tight closet.

After the install: the first year

Set the thermostat at a level that is hot enough for the dishwasher and safe for children. Flush a few quarts from the drain valve once a year to move sediment out. Because local water is moderately hard to hard, per the utility's 2023 report, a yearly flush has real value.
