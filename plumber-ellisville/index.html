Plumbing help for Ellisville, from Manchester Road to Clarkson Road

Ellisville covers about 4.4 square miles and had 9,985 residents in the 2020 census. Two state routes cut through it: Manchester Road, which is Route 100, and Clarkson Road, which is Route 340. Because Ellisville shares a long border with Ballwin, our dispatch point is only minutes away for most addresses, and emergency dispatch is available around the clock.

Ellisville started as a village in 1932, formed to create a public school district, and voters approved fourth class city status in 1957. That history matters to a plumber because neighborhoods grew in stages. A street platted in one decade can have different pipe materials from the next street over, so we ask about the age of your house on the phone.

Before we arrive: if a pipe bursts or a leak is active

Shut off the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. It is usually near the meter or where the line enters the basement.

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for rooms with water near outlets, and stay out of standing water near the panel.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water. Take photos before you start cleanup.

Permits, rules and who to call

Ellisville is its own city with its own government, so permit questions go to the city and not to Ballwin. Call the city before work that involves a new water heater location, a sewer line dig or a basement bathroom. We follow the answer you get.

For sewage backups, remember that, according to the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District, owners are responsible for the entire private building sewer. If neighbors are backed up at the same time, report it to MSD at 1 (866) 281-5737. Our sewer line repair page explains the next steps when a camera shows damage.

Water quality and your fixtures

Missouri American Water's 2023 report for the St. Louis County system lists total hardness of 66 to 226 ppm as calcium carbonate. If that utility supplies your address, you can expect moderate to heavy scale. Test your own tap to know where you stand, since hardness varies by plant.

Scale shortens the life of heaters and clogs aerators, which is why many calls we take in Ellisville start with a fixture that has lost flow. See water softener and filtration for options.
