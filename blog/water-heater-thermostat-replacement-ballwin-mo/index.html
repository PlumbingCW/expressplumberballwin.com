Hot Water Heater Thermostat Replacement for Ballwin, MO Homes

How electric water heater thermostats work, how to test them and when to call.

An electric water heater has two thermostats, and when one fails you get water that is cold, lukewarm or far too hot. Replacing a thermostat is a modest repair, but it involves 240 volt power, so it is not a job for guesswork.

This guide explains how the thermostats work, how to tell a bad one from a bad element, and how to decide between a repair and a new tank. For emergency calls, the water heater repair page lists what we do on site.

How the two thermostats work

An electric tank has an upper and a lower heating element, and each has its own thermostat behind a metal cover on the side of the tank. The upper thermostat acts first. It sends power to the upper element until the top of the tank is hot, then passes power to the lower element.

That handoff matters when you diagnose a problem. If the upper thermostat fails, the lower element may never get power and the whole tank stays cold. If the lower thermostat fails, you get a short supply of hot water that runs out quickly.

Safe checks before you call

What you can do without opening anything

  • ✓Look at the breaker and reset it once if it has tripped
  • ✓Check the water temperature setting on the dial if you can see one
  • ✓Note the model number and the age on the data label
  • ✓Look for water at the base of the tank, since a leak changes the plan
  • ✓Never open the access cover with the breaker on

Access covers hide live wiring. Switch off the breaker and confirm with a tester that power is off before any cover comes off. If you are not comfortable doing that, stop and call.

Thermostat, element or a tired tank

A thermostat usually costs far less than a heating element, and an element costs far less than a new tank. The order of testing matters. A plumber checks power at the terminals, then the thermostat, then the elements with a meter.

If the tank is ten years old or more, has rust at the base or is leaking, replacing the thermostat may only buy a short delay. In that case water heater replacement is the better use of money, and we show both prices.

Setting the temperature

A commonly recommended setting is around 120 degrees, which balances comfort with scald safety and slows mineral buildup. Higher settings raise the scald risk, especially for young children and older adults. Test the water at a tap with a thermometer, since the dial on the tank is only a guide.

If your dishwasher manual calls for hotter water, check whether it has a built-in booster before raising the tank setting for the whole house.

When it is time to call a plumber

Call when the breaker keeps tripping, when you see scorch marks or melted wire insulation, when there is water on the floor, or when you do not own a tester. Electrical faults on a water heater cause fires, and wet wiring is dangerous.

We can usually carry common thermostats on the truck. The dispatcher at (314) 461-6239 will ask for the brand, the voltage and the model number to plan the visit.

What to expect on the bill and the timeline

Thermostat work is usually quick once the cause is confirmed. The plumber tests power, tests the thermostat and the elements, replaces the failed part and checks the water temperature at a tap. Most visits end the same day, and common parts are often carried on the truck.

Costs depend on the part, the time of day and whether a second fault turns up, such as a shorted element or damaged wiring. Ask for the price after the diagnosis and before the replacement, and keep the invoice for your records. If the tank is old, ask for the repair price and the replacement price side by side so you can compare them without pressure.

A final tip: write the date of the repair on the tank with a marker. When the next problem appears, you will know how long the part lasted, and that helps any plumber judge whether the tank still has life left.

Hiring for water heater work in Ballwin

The City of Ballwin requires contractors working in the city to hold a Ballwin contractor license. It also requires an active St. Louis County license in their trade, proof of workers' compensation insurance or an exemption affidavit, and it says electrical, plumbing and mechanical permits carry special restrictions.

Since November 1, 2025, the city says contractor and permit applications go through its MyGov portal. Its building permit page does not address water heater swaps directly, so call the Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 if you are unsure whether yours needs one.

Ask any plumber to show the license before work starts. Homeowners in Manchester and Ellisville should check with their own city, since each runs its own rules. These details are according to the City of Ballwin contractor license page, so confirm current figures there.
