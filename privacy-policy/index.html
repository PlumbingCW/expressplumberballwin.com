Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Ballwin Express Plumbing handles when you visit this website or call our phone line. It was last updated on November 13, 2026.

Call (314) 461-6239

What we collect

This website does not use forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. When you call (314) 461-6239, we may collect your phone number, the details you share about your plumbing problem and your address so we can dispatch a plumber.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded and monitored for quality, training and dispatch accuracy. A call-tracking service may process call details such as the time, duration and caller number. By continuing a call after the notice, you agree to this.

How we use information

  • To dispatch a plumber and give you a price
  • To improve our service and train staff
  • To keep records of work performed
  • To comply with legal obligations

Sharing

We share information with the plumbers who perform the work and with service providers that support our phone system. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you or to delete it, subject to legal and business record requirements. Email contact@expressplumberballwin.com or call (314) 461-6239.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
