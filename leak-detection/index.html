Active leak: shut off the water and start the clock

If water is visibly flowing, shut off the main valve first. It is usually near the water meter or where the line enters the basement. Switch off power to any wet area, move valuables, and photograph the damage.

Many callers do not see water at all. They see a high bill or hear a meter spinning. A plumber can confirm the leak without opening anything, and that is what this page is about.

Meter still running or a high water bill

Turn off every fixture and appliance, then look at the meter. If the dial or low-flow indicator is still moving, water is escaping somewhere between the meter and the fixtures. It could be a toilet flapper, a hose bib, an irrigation line or a buried service line.

Ask your water utility where its responsibility ends and yours begins, because a leak on the customer side of the connection is the owner's repair. Missouri American Water runs a 24/7 line at 1 (866) 269-2837, and a plumber can help you find the main shutoff and the meter.

Quick checks before the plumber arrives

  • ✓Read the meter, wait twenty minutes with all water off, and read it again
  • ✓Add dye tablets or food coloring to each toilet tank and look for color in the bowl
  • ✓Check outdoor spigots and irrigation zone valves for drips
  • ✓Look for warm spots on floors, which can point to a hot-line leak
  • ✓Note the date of the bill spike so the plumber can narrow the window

Ceiling, wall and crawlspace leaks

A brown stain on a ceiling is often a drain or supply line above it, or a leaking shower pan. Water travels along framing before it shows, so the stain is not always under the leak. A moisture map finds the real start point.

Many Ballwin homes have basements, not slabs, so under-floor leaks tend to show up as damp basement walls or wet insulation in a crawlspace. If a supply line cracks inside a wall, the repair may be a short section of new pipe, covered in burst pipe repair.

Buried lines, yards and irrigation

A leak between the curb and the house shows up as a soggy patch of lawn, a sinkhole, or an unexplained drop in pressure. Irrigation leaks often show as a zone that runs wet. A plumber can isolate each section and test it.

Pool and spa plumbing leaks are a minor part of local calls, and we handle plumbing-side leaks that connect to the house system.
