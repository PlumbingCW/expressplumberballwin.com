Wild Wood or Wildwood?

The City of Wildwood is a separate municipality in St. Louis County, with its own government and its own permit office. Wild Wood is in Ballwin. If you search for a plumber and see Wildwood results, confirm the city before you book, because permit and utility questions go to different offices.

Common ground, trustees and your plumbing

A subdivision with five common grounds and a pond means many lots back onto shared land. That matters for a few plumbing jobs. A sump pump discharge, a new hose bib line or a drainage fix should send water away from the foundation without running onto a neighbor's lot or the common ground. Ask your trustees what the indentures allow before you install anything outside.

Call 811 before any digging, including a fence post or a yard line repair, so underground utilities are marked first. For basement water, see sump pump repair and basement waterproofing.

  1. 01

    One slow fixture

    Try a plunger at that fixture first. The problem is probably local.

  2. 02

    Two or more slow fixtures

    Run water in the lowest tub or floor drain. If it gurgles or rises, the main line may be blocked.

  3. 03

    Water coming up in a floor drain

    Stop using water and call. Do not open the cleanout if water is standing high in it.

For a main line blockage, see sewer line cleaning and drain cleaning.

Who owns what

MSD owns the public sewer main. The private building sewer, from the foundation to that main, belongs to the property owner, per MSD. If a neighbor reports a backup on the same day, report it to MSD at 1 (866) 281-5737 as well as calling us.

Ballwin residents with a repeat blockage or a structural defect in the private sewer may be able to apply to the city's Sewer Lateral Repair Program ($150 application, $4,500 job cap, $28 annual fee on the tax bill). It is not specific to Wild Wood, and the Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 can confirm eligibility.

Water quality and water heaters

In its 2023 report for the St. Louis County system, Missouri American Water lists hardness of 66 to 226 ppm as calcium carbonate, which is moderate to heavy. If it supplies your address, scale builds up inside heaters and on fixtures. Because house ages vary, check the date on your water heater's label. See water heater replacement if it is old or leaking.
