Signs your supply pipes are wearing out

Supply pipes rarely fail without warning. Pressure drops at the far end of the house, water runs brown for a minute after sitting, or a pinhole leak shows in a wall or the basement ceiling. When a second pinhole follows the first, the pipe has usually thinned along its whole length.

Common signs

  • ✓Low water pressure that cleaning aerators does not fix
  • ✓Rusty or discolored water from the tap
  • ✓Pinhole leaks or green stains on copper
  • ✓Visible corrosion on exposed basement lines
  • ✓Repeated repairs in the same run

Whole house or one section

A partial repipe replaces the worst run, such as a kitchen branch or the line to a second-floor bath. It costs less and fits a tight budget. A whole-house repipe replaces everything from the main to every fixture, and it is worth considering if leaks keep returning in different places.

A new main line from the meter is a separate decision. The utility and the owner each own part of the service connection, so ask Missouri American Water where your section starts. If the leak is between the curb and the house, leak detection confirms where it is first.

How a repipe is done

  1. 01

    Survey

    The plumber maps existing lines, pressure and fixtures, then proposes a route.

  2. 02

    Rough in

    New lines are run through walls, floors and the basement with as few openings as possible.

  3. 03

    Connect and test

    Fixtures are reconnected and the system is pressure tested.

  4. 04

    Patch and clean

    Small access openings are covered for drywall repair, and the work area is cleaned.

Water service is usually off for part of a day. We plan the order so a kitchen or bath stays usable where we can, and we tell you the schedule ahead.

Permits and inspection

The City of Ballwin says plumbing permits are typically needed for work such as basement finishing and that building permits apply to construction. Call Building Inspections at (636) 227-2129 to confirm what your repipe needs. We follow the answer.
