How we decide who comes to your address

The dispatcher asks for your street and a cross street, then looks at where crews are working. Distance matters less than timing. A crew finishing a job two miles away may reach you sooner than one leaving the office, so the arrival estimate you hear is based on the actual schedule.

That is also why we ask what kind of building you have and what the problem looks like. A basement flood needs pumps, a sewer backup needs a cable machine and a failed water heater needs a replacement unit. Sending the right truck once beats sending the nearest truck twice.

Not sure if we cover your street? Call and ask the dispatcher.

Local rules differ from town to town

Each of these places is its own city with its own building office. Permit rules, contractor licensing and inspection requirements are set locally, so we send you to the right office rather than guessing. For Ballwin, the Building Inspections Department answers questions at (636) 227-2129.

Sewer ownership follows the same rule across St. Louis County. According to the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District, owners are responsible for the private building sewer, and it takes reports of public main problems at 1 (866) 281-5737. Our service pages explain what to do in each case.
