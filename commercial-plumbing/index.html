A business plumbing emergency: the first steps

Downtime costs a business money, so the first minutes matter. Find the main water shutoff and close it if water is spreading. Move stock, electronics and paper records away from the wet area, and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Tell the dispatcher what kind of business you run, whether customers are on site, and which fixtures are down. A restaurant with a failed grease trap needs a different crew plan than an office with a dripping ceiling.

Before we arrive

  • ✓Shut off the main or the zone valve nearest the problem
  • ✓Turn off power to equipment in standing water
  • ✓Keep customers and staff out of wet areas
  • ✓Locate floor drains, cleanouts and the grease interceptor
  • ✓Call your landlord or property manager if you lease the space

Ballwin's commercial corridors and what they need

Manchester Road is Ballwin's main commercial street, and our own address is on it. Restaurants, offices and shops along the corridor often sit in multi-tenant buildings. If your unit shares walls or a sewer line with a neighbor, say so when you call, because a backup in one unit can reach the next.

The City of Ballwin says that building permits are required for construction, and that its Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 handles the process. For tenant improvements and new fixtures, confirm permit needs with the city and your landlord before work begins.

Backflow, water heaters and routine service

Many commercial buildings have backflow preventers that must be tested on a schedule set by the water utility or the city. Ask your utility or Ballwin which devices and testing frequency apply to your building. We can repair or replace the device, and our plumbers can advise on the schedule.

Commercial water heaters run longer hours than home units. See the water heater replacement page for how we handle a failed unit, and plan service before a busy season rather than during it.
