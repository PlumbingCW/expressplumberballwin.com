Faucet spraying or will not shut off: stop the water first

Look under the sink for the two small oval or round stop valves on the supply lines and turn each clockwise until it stops. If they will not turn or keep leaking, go to the main water shutoff and close it. Put a bucket under the leak and dry the cabinet floor, since particleboard cabinets swell quickly.

What a dripping faucet is telling you

A steady drip wastes water and raises the bill. The cause depends on the faucet type. Older compression faucets wear a rubber washer. Cartridge, ball and ceramic disc faucets wear a cartridge or seals.

Where the water appears also matters. A drip from the spout means the internal seal has failed. Water around the base or the handle points to worn O-rings. A leak under the sink may be a supply line or a drain fitting rather than the faucet.

Why Ballwin faucets lose flow

Missouri American Water's 2023 St. Louis County report lists total hardness of 66 to 226 ppm as calcium carbonate. That range is moderately hard to hard. Minerals collect in the aerator screen and inside cartridges, which cuts flow and makes handles stiff.

Unscrew the aerator and soak it in vinegar to see if flow returns. If it does not, the cartridge or the stop valve is the likely cause. A water softener reduces the buildup throughout the house.

Repair or replace

A faucet that is ten years old or more and needs a cartridge may be near the end of its life. Parts for discontinued models can be hard to find. We tell you the cost of the repair next to the cost of a new faucet, so you can choose.

If you are updating a bathroom or kitchen, ask about replacing the shutoff valves under the sink at the same time. Old stops can seize or leak when disturbed. The plumbing installation page covers fixture upgrades.

A quick check before the plumber arrives

Close the stop valves under the sink if the drip is heavy. Put a bowl or towel under the supply lines to see whether the leak is from the faucet or from a connection. Note the brand stamped on the faucet, since it speeds the parts search.

Standards note: Replacement faucets should meet the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and carry a mark showing they meet lead-free requirements. The City of Ballwin lists the International Plumbing Code among its adopted codes, and the Building Inspections Department at (636) 227-2129 can confirm which edition applies.
